## Scope and mode

`/together` is an Operate-mode, customer-facing interactive prototype extending the established
Lookline interface.

## Audience and job

Hackathon judges and prospective customers need to understand and operate the complete physical-first
journey without seeing internal system details.

## Primary tasks and content

- Walk a live catalog product through Ready Now discovery, preview, purchase and Look Card issue.
- Turn unmet intent and optional reference imagery into a base-pattern-led Made for You review.
- Borrow an owner-shared purchase digitally, see a Circle offer and create a separate owned order.
- Compare the before-purchase, after-purchase and Circle milestones across all three paths.

## Direction

A judges' tour inside The Rack: a title, one line, three path tabs, a compact five-step stepper, and the
product stage as the largest element. Every helper sentence is one short line or gone; sample fulfilment,
quote and offer states carry a small "Sample" tag. The tour is reached from the footer ("Prototype tour"),
not from the shopper's navigation.

## Constraints

Use live catalog products and honest unavailable states. Label sample quotes, offers and fulfillment
states. No free recoloring, pre-purchase card ownership, invented purchasable variants or internal
ontology, model, graph, cost, risk or offer-construction data. Responsive and keyboard-accessible.

## Unresolved

Production custom-SKU, quote, rights-review, fulfillment and offer integrations remain beyond the
hackathon; the surface demonstrates only their customer-visible state contract.
