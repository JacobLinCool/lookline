## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Makalot hackathon judges viewing a 3–5 minute product presentation.

## Product Purpose

Extend a single apparel purchase into persistent styling, creation and sharing. The user describes this as moving from a point, to a line, to a plane.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Shopping finds the desired or missing item in the context of the existing wardrobe. Full outfit composition belongs in the digital wardrobe, where the user can compose freely and request AI assistance. The two loops connect through owned garments. Generated previews are asynchronous. Below 0.5 seconds is a proposed interaction target, not an established measurement. Friend items can be tried in compositions without implying ownership. Shared works travel through existing social platforms. Structured preference feedback and unmet demand inform subsequent recommendations and potential product development.

## Brand Commitments

The user confirmed LookLine as the presentation name. Traditional Chinese is the primary presentation language.
